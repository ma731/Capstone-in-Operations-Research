The towers show real 2025 hourly averages. The moving jobs are a picture of the idea; every percentage is measured by the full scheduler on held-out 2025 data.

One layer pays. Two mostly do not.

The question was never whether carbon-aware scheduling works; the industry already does it. It was how sophisticated the model needs to be. Each layer was added one at a time to the same scheduler, with the test rules written down before the 2025 data was opened.

  • Fund the lever that paysLetting flexible AI jobs move between regions is an operating decision: a scheduler and a policy for which jobs may move.
  • Skip what does notJoint dependence models and robust optimisation need specialist skills and slower solves, and added almost nothing on real grids.
  • Be honest about sizeAt a carbon price alone: about EUR 2k to 6k a year for 5 MW of flexible compute, EUR 49k to 120k for 100 MW.
