    The price of sophistication

    A scheduler can send AI compute to wherever the power is cleanest. How much modelling does that actually need? I tested it on a year of real grid data from eleven regions of North America.

          Not worth it

          One layer pays. Two mostly do not.

          Each layer was added on its own to the same scheduler, with the test rules fixed before the 2025 data was opened. Western US, at real grid conditions:

            • Fund the lever that paysLetting flexible AI jobs move between regions is an operating decision: a scheduler and a policy for which jobs may move.
            • Skip what does notDependence models and robust optimisation need specialist skills and slower solves, and added almost nothing on real grids.
            • Be honest about the sizeAt a carbon price alone: about EUR 2k to 6k a year for 5 MW of flexible compute, EUR 49k to 120k for 100 MW.
